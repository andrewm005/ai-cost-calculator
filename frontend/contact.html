What to write about

Any of these — and we read every message:

Wrong or stale price

If a number on the site disagrees with the vendor's current pricing page, send the model name, the price you saw, and the URL of the vendor's page. Re-fetch cycle is 6 hours; serious bugs are fast-tracked.

Missing model

If a model you use isn't on the site, send the model name and ideally a link to its pricing page. New-model requests are reviewed weekly.

Calculator bug

If the math looks wrong (input × rate ≠ output), include the model, the workload, and the number you expected. Math is verified against a second implementation in tests; bugs are rare but real.

Privacy, GDPR, or data request

The privacy policy describes what we do and don't collect. Data-subject requests are answered within 30 days as required.

Partnership, press, or commercial use

Including requests to license calculator output, embed the widget, or syndicate model pages.

Security disclosure

Coordinated disclosure is welcome. We aim to acknowledge within 3 days and resolve critical issues within 30 days.

What we don't answer

  • Vendor-specific pricing questions (we are not affiliated with any provider; please contact the vendor directly).
  • Requests for bulk data exports or scraping permission beyond what's already public at api.aicostcalculator.net/models.
  • SEO, link-exchange, or guest-post requests. The site's editorial direction is decided by the operator.
  • Anything that needs a same-day reply. If your deadline is less than 24 hours, the calculator's number is good enough — verify with the vendor before relying on it.

Why there's no contact form

Most sites put a contact form on this page. We don't. The site's stated principle — repeated on the privacy policy — is no tracking, no analytics, no cookies, no email collection. A backend contact form would mean building a data pipeline for messages, choosing what to log, and committing to keeping it secure. A mailto: link plus a real inbox is simpler, more honest, and avoids the question entirely. Your message goes directly to the operator's email client; nothing on our end stores it before or after we read it.

If you'd rather not open a mail client, copy the address and paste it into whatever you normally use: help@aicostcalculator.net.

Response time

Realistic answer: most messages get a personal reply within 1 to 3 days. We aim to acknowledge within 7 days even if the full answer will take longer. Pricing-bug reports and security disclosures get priority. Anything sent during a US holiday week will take longer; the operator is one person.

Other places to reach us

  • About — who runs the site, methodology, and the public source code repository.
  • GitHub — bug reports and feature requests via issues on the public repository.
  • LinkedIn — for partnership and press only; the inbox above is faster for everything else.

The contact page is intentionally a single email address and a handful of clear promises. If you need something from us, the path is short.
