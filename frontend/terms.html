What the service is

AI Cost Calculator is a free, no-signup web tool that estimates the cost of running AI workloads across a curated list of large language models. The calculator multiplies model pricing (input tokens, output tokens, cached input, reasoning tokens, tool calls, image input) by a workload that you specify, then displays a dollar figure.

The site also publishes model-specific reference pages, side-by-side comparison pages, and a blog with longer-form articles about AI token economics. All of those are part of "the service" for the purposes of these terms.

Pricing data and accuracy

Most pricing comes from OpenRouter's public /api/v1/models endpoint, refreshed by our backend every six hours. A smaller set of entries comes from a hand-curated JSON file that we cross-check against each vendor's published pricing page on the same cycle.

Pricing on third-party services can be wrong, stale, or change without notice. Specifically:

  • OpenRouter's feed includes a small platform fee on top of upstream vendor list prices (typically a few percent). Vendor-direct pricing may differ.
  • Some providers update their public pricing faster than OpenRouter does. Our cache can lag the actual list price by up to six hours.
  • Regional pricing varies. The numbers shown are the headline US/global list price, not your specific contract or region.
  • Batch API discounts (typically 50% off for OpenAI and Anthropic) are not modeled by the calculator.
  • Enterprise contracts, committed-use discounts, credits, and rebates are not modeled.
  • Fine-tuning, embeddings, image generation at variable resolution, and self-hosted GPU cost are partially or fully out of scope (see Methodology).

Because of all of the above, the dollar figures shown by the calculator are estimates, not quotes. Do not commit to a price, a budget, or a contract based on a number from this site without confirming it against the vendor's own pricing page first. The disclaimer page covers this in more detail.
