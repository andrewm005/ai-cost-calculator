What "estimate" means here

The calculator returns a dollar figure by multiplying a model price (dollars per million tokens, with separate rates for input, output, cached input, reasoning, and image input) by a workload that you specify. The arithmetic is simple; the inputs to it are imperfect. The site operator is not a reseller, broker, or representative of any provider listed on this site. The numbers shown are what each vendor publishes, rounded to two decimals, with no markup from us.

What the calculator does not know about your situation

Specific things that will change the real-world bill but are not modeled:

  • Batch API discounts. OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google offer 50% off for batch workloads. The calculator uses the on-demand rate.
  • Enterprise contracts and committed-use discounts. Negotiated rates, multi-year commitments, and credits are private to each account.
  • Regional pricing. Different data-residency regions, EU-only endpoints, and sovereign-cloud offerings are priced separately.
  • Promotional credits. New-account credits, referral credits, and free-tier allowances are not subtracted from the displayed total.
  • Per-image variance. Image-input cost is calculated at a flat per-image estimate, not the actual multi-resolution cost for the specific model you're using.
  • Fine-tuning cost. Custom-model training, hosted fine-tuning, and embedding-model fees are out of scope.
  • Self-hosted GPU cost. The /calculate/local endpoint gives a rough number, but it is a rough number; actual rental rates and idle time vary.
  • Tool-call overhead beyond the preset. The default workload assumes a 2,000-token system prompt and 5 tool calls per loop. Your actual loops may differ.
  • Refund, dispute, or chargeback outcomes. Not modeled.
  • Currency conversion. Figures are US dollars. Your card may be charged in a different currency at the rate your card issuer applies.

How stale the price might be

OpenRouter's feed is re-fetched every six hours. Hand-curated entries for direct-API vendors are cross-checked on the same cycle. There is therefore a window of up to six hours during which the displayed price can differ from the vendor's current list price. The "Last updated" timestamp shown on each model page is the timestamp of the most recent refresh for that page.

If a model disappears from the upstream feed between refreshes, it disappears from the calculator on the next refresh. We do not invent replacement prices. If a model's price changes upstream and the change hasn't yet propagated to our cache, the displayed price is the old one. Trust the vendor's page over the calculator for any decision made inside that window.
